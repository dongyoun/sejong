1. Purpose

These terms set the conditions for using HyperSeven Office and HyperSeven CAD and the HyperSeven account and AI subscription services (the "Service"), provided by HYSTERESIS (the "Operator") under the name HyperSeven Labs.

2. The Service

  1. All editing and drawing features of the apps are free and work without an account. The AI assistant shows what it does in a preview (a scripted scene, not the real AI).
  2. Paid services are per app: Support (monthly or one-time, section 4-2) and Subscription (HyperAI, monthly, section 4-3). AI subscriptions that use the Operator's AI (section 4) are not sold at present. A purchase for one app does not cover the other.
  3. A HyperSeven account is optional. One account signs you in to both apps, and subscriptions and support belong to that account.
  4. AI is provided only as a feature inside the apps. AI usage (tokens or credits) is not sold separately.
  5. The Operator may improve and extend the Service. Changes to paid services that are unfavorable to users are announced in advance under section 12.

3. Account

  1. Accounts are created with Apple or Google sign-in. The Operator receives only the name, email and unique ID that provider shares.
  2. Keep your devices and account secure. If you suspect someone else used it, sign out of all devices and tell the Operator.
  3. A subscription is for the person who subscribed. Accounts may not be shared or resold.

4. AI subscription

  1. An AI subscription is a monthly subscription that uses the Operator's AI. It is not sold at present. Before it opens we announce it under section 12 and show prices and contents per app on the pricing page and on the subscription and checkout screens. Korean won prices include VAT; US dollar prices exclude tax, which is added for your country at checkout. Paragraphs 2 onward of this section also apply to Subscriptions (section 4-3).
  2. Subscriptions renew automatically every month from the payment date, charging the same payment method. Before each renewal we email you the date and amount, and the renewal date is always shown in the app's account panel.
  3. Each subscription has a fair-use limit per billing period. When it is used up, you continue at the next lower intelligence level; when that share is used up too, AI requests pause until the next renewal and the app tells you when they resume. The share you have used is always visible in the app.
  4. You can change level at any time. The price difference is prorated and charged or credited immediately.
  5. You can cancel at any time from Manage subscription in the app. No further payments are taken, and you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  6. If a payment fails, the payment provider retries and you keep access for up to 3 days. If payment still fails, the subscription is paused.
  7. Price increases are announced by email and in the app at least 30 days in advance and apply only from the renewal after you agree. If you do not agree, the subscription ends at the end of the current period.

4-2. Support

  1. Support makes you a HyperSeven supporter. It comes as Monthly support (Supporter membership) and One-time support (the Supporter Pack).
  2. Supporter perks are: early access to new features (screen updates before they open to everyone), priority answers to your support emails, and a supporter badge in the app. Monthly support gives them for the paid period; One-time support for one year from payment. Early-access features may be less polished than the general release.
  3. Monthly support is a monthly subscription. Prices differ per app (Office $3 / ₩3,900, CAD $5 / ₩6,900) and are shown on the pricing page and at checkout. Automatic renewal, cancellation, failed payments and price increases follow section 4, paragraphs 2, 5, 6 and 7.
  4. One-time support is a single payment. You choose the amount, at or above the minimum for each app (Office $5 / ₩6,900, CAD $7 / ₩9,900).
  5. Support is processed as a product purchase, not a donation. No donation receipt is issued; the payment provider sends the receipt. App features and AI terms outside the supporter perks are the same with or without support.

4-3. Subscription

  1. A Subscription turns on the app's HyperAI (AI connection) with your own AI API key, billed monthly. Prices differ per app (Office $5 / ₩6,900, CAD $10 / ₩13,900) and are shown on the pricing page and at checkout. AI usage is not included; you pay the AI company directly.
  2. The prices and terms of the AI company you connect are between you and that company.
  3. New users get 7 days free without subscribing (free trial). The trial is once per app and starts when you sign in to that app after paid plans open. No payment method is collected; after the trial, HyperAI works only with a subscription.
  4. Until paid plans open, the subscription features are free.
  5. Automatic renewal, cancellation, failed payments and price increases follow section 4, paragraphs 2, 5, 6 and 7.

5. Payment

  1. Payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) as reseller and merchant of record. Paddle handles payment methods, receipts and taxes; the Operator never receives card numbers or similar payment details.
  2. Depending on your country, Paddle may add taxes; the final amount is shown at checkout.

6. Withdrawal and refunds

  1. You may withdraw within 7 days of payment.
  2. Support and Subscriptions are fully refunded within 7 days of payment. Once AI subscriptions open, a billing period's usage limit is digital content divided by use, so the part already used cannot be withdrawn and the unused share is refunded (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 17(2)(v), proviso).
  3. What cannot be withdrawn is stated on the pricing page and at checkout before payment, and the AI preview lets you see what the assistant does first (same Article, paragraph 6).
  4. After 7 days a subscription can only be cancelled; you keep access until the period ends (section 4.5).
  5. If the AI assistant was unavailable for a substantial part of a billing period through the Operator's fault (for example an outage), the unavailable days are refunded pro rata.
  6. Request refunds by email (see Seller information below). Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paddle. Where Paddle's buyer terms give wider refund rights in your country, those apply.

7. AI assistant

  1. AI answers can be wrong. Check facts, numbers, legal, medical and other important content before applying it. The app only proposes edits; your document changes only when you press Apply.
  2. To process an AI request, your request and as much of the document as needed are sent to Anthropic (Claude). The Operator does not store request or response text; it records only usage (token counts and cost).
  3. HyperAI works only through the AI assistant inside the apps. It may not be used to break the law or Anthropic's usage policies, to infringe others' rights, to call the AI in bulk through automation, or to use the Service outside the apps.
  4. If these rules are broken, the Operator may block the request or suspend the account. The remaining days of the subscription are then refunded pro rata, minus any damage the violation caused the Operator.

8. Your rights and documents

  1. Rights to the documents you create and to AI-generated output belong to you. The Operator does not store your documents in your account.
  2. Documents are saved on your computer. Keep your own backups of important documents.

9. Operator's rights

Rights to the apps, the Service and the name HyperSeven belong to the Operator. Open-source components included in the apps follow their own licenses (Open source licenses in the app).
