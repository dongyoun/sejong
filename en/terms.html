  3. A HyperSeven account is optional. One account signs you in to both apps, and subscriptions belong to that account.

3. Account

  1. Accounts are created with Apple or Google sign-in. The Operator receives only the name, email and unique ID that provider shares.
  2. Keep your devices and account secure. If you suspect someone else used it, sign out of all devices and tell the Operator.
  3. A subscription is for the person who subscribed. Accounts may not be shared or resold.

4. AI subscription

  1. The subscription fee is a fixed monthly amount per app, shown on the subscription and checkout screens. Displayed prices include VAT.
  2. Subscriptions renew automatically every month from the payment date, charging the same payment method. The renewal date is shown in the app's account panel.
  3. Each subscription has a fair-use allowance per billing period — generous for ordinary document work. When it is used up, AI requests pause until the next renewal, and the app tells you when they resume. The share you have used is always visible in the app.
  4. You can cancel at any time from Manage subscription in the app. No further payments are taken, and you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  5. If a payment fails, the payment provider retries and you keep access for up to 3 days. If payment still fails, the subscription is paused.
  6. Price increases are announced by email and in the app at least 30 days in advance and apply only from the renewal after you agree. If you do not agree, the subscription ends at the end of the current period.

  1. Within 7 days of a payment, if you have not used the AI assistant in that billing period, you get a full refund.
  2. Once the AI assistant has been used in a billing period, the digital service has been provided, so that period cannot be withdrawn. This is stated on the subscription screen before payment. You can still cancel, and you keep access until the period ends (section 4.4).
  3. If the AI assistant was unavailable for a substantial part of a billing period through the Operator's fault (for example an outage), the unavailable days are refunded pro rata.
  4. Request refunds by email (see Seller information below). Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paddle. Where Paddle's buyer terms give wider refund rights in your country, those apply.

  4. If these rules are broken, the Operator may block the request or suspend the account. The remaining days of the subscription are then refunded pro rata, minus any damage the violation caused the Operator.
