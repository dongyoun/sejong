Refund Policy

Payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) as merchant of record, and refunds go back to your original payment method through Paddle. Full conditions are in section 6 of the Terms of Service.

Within 7 days

  • Not used: full refund — for the Supporter Pack, Connect and AI subscriptions alike.
  • Partly used: the unused share is refunded. The Supporter Pack's AI taste and an AI subscription's monthly usage limit are digital content divided by use, so the part already used cannot be refunded (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 17(2)(v), proviso).
  • The share you have used is always shown in the app's account panel.

After 7 days

  • You can cancel a subscription at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period. No further payments are taken.
  • If the AI was unavailable for a substantial part of a billing period through our fault (for example an outage), the unavailable days are refunded pro rata.
  • If a paid service is discontinued, we announce it 30 days in advance and refund the remaining days pro rata.

How to request

  • Send us an email with the account you paid with and your receipt number. We usually handle it within 2 business days.
  • You can also use the link in Paddle's receipt email.
  • Where Paddle's buyer terms give wider refund rights in your country, those apply.

Good to know

  • Every editing and drawing feature of the apps is free. Before paying you can see what the AI does in the in-app preview.
  • The Supporter Pack is a product with a thank-you gift (an AI taste); it is processed as a purchase, and no donation receipt is issued. Support via Buy Me a Coffee or Bitcoin is a donation, not a product, and is outside this policy.
