Corner Office

A six-storey office where two streets meet: a glass corner and vertical louvres, core and lifts, a basement parking ramp, a sixth-floor terrace and a roof garden. Every schedule and every plan, section and elevation sheet comes from this one model.

Opens in HyperSeven CAD, ready to edit. In the current release it opens at the default view instead of its start scene, and the louvre and lift tables on the schedule sheet (A-601) do not display correctly. Both are right from the next release.

Making-of notes

HyperSeven CAD can rebuild the model from scratch from 1,348 commands. Rebuilding and saving took 0.30 s, and opening the file through to the first 3D view took 0.4 s (Apple M5 Pro, 24 GB memory, macOS 27.2.1, HyperSeven CAD development build).

The 2,021 products exported to IFC4 passed ifcopenshell 0.8.4 schema validation with 0 errors (2026-11-22).

Added to the app while building this model: lift and vertical-louvre components, exporting placed components with their IFC category classes, curtain walls spanning several floors now bound rooms on every floor, refined scale figures and cars, and 3D views as start views.

Trees, people, cars and furniture are HyperSeven CAD library components, and the neighbouring buildings are simple masses. No third-party 3D models were used.

B1Freeform

Shell Pavilion

A timber-and-glass gridshell roof, about 42 × 30 m, over a park plaza. The app's Divide Surface splits a single NURBS base surface into 1,728 triangular panels, and a panel schedule counts them with their area and flatness. Because they are triangles, every panel is flat. Slender columns and inclined edge beams carry the roof, with a glass kiosk and curved benches, modelled in the app with SubD, underneath.

The current release cannot draw this model's roof panels (NURBS Divide Surface), so the roof looks empty. The roof panels and the panel schedule open in HyperSeven CAD from the next release. The IFC, 3DM and SKP files contain every panel.

Making-of notes

HyperSeven CAD can rebuild the model from scratch from 231 commands. Rebuilding and saving took 0.14 s, and opening the file through to the first 3D view took 0.3 s (Apple M5 Pro, 24 GB memory, macOS 27.2.1, HyperSeven CAD development build).

The 258 products exported to IFC4 passed ifcopenshell 0.8.4 schema validation with 0 errors (2026-11-22). The roof panels go out as a single IfcRoof, and the ten curved benches as IfcFurniture.

The base surface (NURBS degree 3 × 3, 7 × 5 control points) sits on a hidden layer named 'Base surface'. Turn the layer on and move a control point, and the panels divide again. The curved bench was modelled once in SubD, grouped as a component and placed ten times; edit the definition and every copy changes.

Added to the app while building this model: Divide Surface now splits NURBS surfaces into even cells, the panel schedule gains area and flatness columns, a command line that writes saved schedules to JSON exactly as the app computes them (supercad info --schedules), and an IFC class set on a component definition now applies to every copy. All of these arrive in the next release of HyperSeven CAD.

Trees and people are HyperSeven CAD library components. No third-party 3D models were used.

B2Freeform

Twisted Tower

A 36-storey octagonal tower that turns 1.5° and narrows on every floor. One node graph builds the floors, glazing, core, stairs, rooms, spiral corner members and roof; change a value and rebake, and the tower rebuilds itself. Every element sits on its own level, so the floor-area schedule and the IFC storeys match.

Opens in HyperSeven CAD, ready to edit. The current release does not know the graph's stair and room nodes, so rebaking does not work correctly, and it shows false slab-overlap warnings. Both work from the next release.

Making-of notes

HyperSeven CAD can rebuild the model from scratch from 131 commands, graph baking included. Rebuilding and saving took 0.17 s, and opening the file through to the first 3D view took 0.3 s (Apple M5 Pro, 24 GB memory, macOS 27.2.1, HyperSeven CAD development build).

The 5,393 products exported to IFC4 passed ifcopenshell 0.8.4 schema validation with 0 errors (2026-11-22). Floors, glazing, walls and spiral members each sit in their own IFC storey.

Added to the app while building this model: 'level auto' for node-graph baking (elements made at each height go to their own level), 'stair' and 'room' nodes (change the floor count, rebake, and the stairs, rooms and core walls follow), saved views that restore their field of view, and a slab-overlap check that understands openings.

The lifts and shafts, the plaza, canopy and city context were placed with commands. Trees, people and lifts are HyperSeven CAD library components. No third-party 3D models were used.
