Good to know

  • US dollar prices exclude tax, which is added for your country at checkout. In Korea prices are in won and include VAT. Paddle processes payments as our reseller and merchant of record — cards, PayPal and more; KakaoPay and Naver Pay in Korea.
  • Connect and monthly support are per app and renew monthly. We remind you before renewal; if you cancel, everything stays as it is until the end of the paid period.
  • Create an API key at console.anthropic.com. Sign in and add it once; it stays in that computer's keychain and never goes to HyperSeven's servers.
  • Support (monthly or one-time) is processed as a purchase, not a donation, and no donation receipt is issued.
  • Within 7 days of payment you get a full refund. See the refund policy.
  • Until paid plans open, Connect is free, and you can support with a coffee or Bitcoin.
